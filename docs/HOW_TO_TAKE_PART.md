# How to take part (even if you've never used GitHub)

GitHub is a website where software projects live. You don't need to code to join in here.

## 1. Make a free account (one minute)
Go to [github.com/signup](https://github.com/signup). That's all you need to comment.

## 2. Say hello, ask, or give feedback in Discussions
Open the **[Discussions](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-commons/discussions)** tab. It works like a forum:
- **Q&A**: ask anything about AZOTH.
- **Ideas**: suggest something for the Hive to make or try.
- **Show and tell**: share what you made with something from AZOTH (with credit).
- **General**: anything else.

## 3. Ask permission to use something
Fill in the **[permission request form](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-commons/issues/new?template=permission_request.yml)**:
say what you want to use and how. The owner answers personally.

## 4. Report a problem
Found something broken, like a link, a video or a tool? [Open an issue](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-commons/issues/new/choose).

## 5. Want to collaborate on the full build?
Introduce yourself in Discussions: who you are, and what you'd like to work on. The full Hive lives in a private
repository, and collaborators are invited one at a time.

## Ground rules
Be kind. Credit people. Don't post anyone's private information. Material here is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 unless it says otherwise.
