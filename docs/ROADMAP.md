# Roadmap

*Where AZOTH is heading. The order can change; being honest about status won't.*

## Now (autumn 2026)
- **Episode 0: "What is AZOTH?"**: a narrated short documentary the Hive makes from its own script.
- **The Hive drives its own productions**: give it a screenplay or song sheet and it makes the whole thing locally.
- **The Tube Hare**: finishing the hero character (outfit, expressive eyes and ears, a better run).
- **Synth World**: rooms where every machine is a real sound-making part, playable from the City.

## Next
- A documentary series: one short film for each part of the Hive and how it grew.
- Music videos cut to the beat, sung songs, and sound effects the Hive makes itself.
- More citizens in full 3D, reusing the Tube Hare's body, face and motion systems.
- Motion capture from a phone video: record a gesture, and a citizen performs it.

## Later
- **An installer**: one download that sets up the whole Hive on your own computer.
- Your own City, which grows differently from ours, and maybe, one day, Cities that can visit each other.

Follow progress in [Discussions](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-commons/discussions).
