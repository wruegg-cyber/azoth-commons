# FAQ

**Is this a game?**
Not exactly. It's a creative studio you explore like a game: you walk around a 3D city, and the characters there make
things.

**Can I download and run it?**
Not yet. The full Hive needs local AI models, a game engine and large free asset libraries. An installer is on the
[Roadmap](ROADMAP.md). For now you can watch, listen and talk with us.

**Is it made by AI?**
It's made by a person working *with* AI. The owner directs, AI assistants help build and check, and the Hive's own local
models make more of the content every week. Every borrowed asset is credited in [CREDITS.md](../CREDITS.md).

**Does it use other people's art or music without permission?**
No. Third-party material is either public domain (CC0) or used under its licence with credit. Anything with unclear
rights is kept out.

**Can I use something from AZOTH?**
The public material is CC BY-NC-SA 4.0: share and adapt it with credit, but not commercially. For anything else,
[ask for permission](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-commons/issues/new?template=permission_request.yml).

**Can I help?**
Yes. Start with [How to take part](HOW_TO_TAKE_PART.md).

**Why does some of it look rough?**
Because it's real and early. We'd rather show a world learning than fake a finished one.
