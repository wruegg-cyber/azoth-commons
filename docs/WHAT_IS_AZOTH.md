# What is AZOTH?

*A plain-language explanation. No coding knowledge needed.*

## The short answer
AZOTH is an **experimental creative studio that lives on one home computer**. Its heart is **the Hive**: a group of AI
characters, called **citizens**, who live in a small 3D city, remember what happens to them, and make things together:
music, films, pictures, stories, even small AI models of their own.

## Why "the Hive"?
Most AI tools answer a question and forget you. The Hive is the opposite: a **persistent society**. The same citizens
are there tomorrow, with yesterday's memories. They have jobs (musician, archivist, builder...), places they live and
work, and opinions about each other's work.

## Why a city you can walk through?
Because a place is easier to understand than a menu. Each part of the system *lives somewhere*: music in the Music
District, memory in the Archive Cloisters, building in the Forge Ring. You walk there, talk to the citizen who does that
job, and watch the work happen. It is not a game, but it plays like one.

## What makes it different
- **It grows.** The citizens keep improving what they made: *"this is all right, but how could it be better?"*
- **It runs locally.** The citizens think with AI models running on the owner's own computer, not a cloud service.
  They also train small models of their own from free, openly licensed material.
- **It is honest about itself.** The project keeps measured facts (what the system really does) separate from the
  legend it tells about itself, and every claim points to evidence.
- **It is built in the open, rough edges included.** Early videos may be clumsy. That's the story: you can watch the
  world learn.

## Who makes it?
One person, Will ([wruegg-cyber](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber)), a creative director, not a programmer. He works
with a crew of AI assistants that build, check and research, and the Hive itself does more of the work every week.

## Where is it going?
Toward a studio where the Hive can take a script (a screenplay, a song idea, a game idea) and make the whole thing
itself: the music, the film, the voices, the world. See the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md).
