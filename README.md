# AZOTH

**An experimental organism for growing a persistent, self-improving creative world.**

AZOTH is a one-person project (with a crew of AI helpers) building **the Hive** -- a society of local AI citizens who
live in a 3D city on a small planet, keep their own memories, make music together, film themselves, and keep asking of
everything they make: *this is all right, but how could it be better?* It is not a game, but it plays like one: you
walk the City, talk to its citizens, and watch them work.

It is early and rough, on purpose in public: **you are watching a world learn how to become one.**

## What exists today (October 2026)
- A walkable 3D Hive City wrapped around a cube planet, with citizens who have names, jobs, memories and opinions.
- A Hive band that writes and performs songs; a Hive film pipeline that shoots the live City (first devlog: 60 s).
- Local models the Hive trains itself (drum feel, a sprite grown from a single seed, physics sense) -- small and honest.
- A written history of the City, the *Chronicle*, kept separate from measured fact.

## What is public here
- This front door, the project catalog and the standards the project follows.
- [AZOTH PRISM](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber/azoth-prism) -- alpha audio-analysis tools (AGPL-3.0).

The Hive, the City, the music and the films are developed privately. Selected pieces are released here as they mature.
