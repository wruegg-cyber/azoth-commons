# Credits

AZOTH is made by Will ([wruegg-cyber](https://github.com/wruegg-cyber)) with the Hive's citizens and a crew of AI
assistants. It stands on free and open work by many people. Thank you.

## Music: "EchoesVeil: Second Light"
- Composed and performed by Hive citizens Petra Pitch, Elias Quill, Vael and Jun Mercer. Their choices were made by a
  local Qwen3-8B model (Apache-2.0).
- **Drum performances** adapted from the **Groove MIDI Dataset v1.0.0**, Google LLC, **CC BY 4.0**. Gillick et al.
  (2019), *Learning to Groove with Inverse Sequence Transformations*. <https://magenta.tensorflow.org/datasets/groove>
  Changes: tempo set to 96 BPM; bars extracted, arranged and velocity-scaled; fills added at section ends.
- **Instrument samples**: Versilian Community Sample Library (**VCSL**), Versilian Studios LLC, **CC0 1.0**.
  <https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL>

## Film: Hive City Devlog 01
- Filmed in the Hive City with the **Godot Engine** (MIT) and its Movie Maker mode; edited with **FFmpeg** (LGPL).
- Music: see above.

## The Tube Hare
- Character design: AZOTH (owner's concept). Garment and shoe bases from **MakeHuman** system assets (**CC0**).
- Motion: walk, run and jump adapted from the **CMU Graphics Lab Motion Capture Database** (<http://mocap.cs.cmu.edu>,
  created with funding from NSF EIA-0196217), plus hand and gesture motion from **Quaternius Universal Animation
  Library** (**CC0**). Raw motion data is not redistributed here.
- Authored in **Blender** (GPL); rendered in Godot.

## The Hive City
- Surface materials and textures from **Poly Haven** and **ambientCG** (**CC0**).

- Growth rule: Google Research *Growing Neural Cellular Automata*

- Target sprite: **Kenney** "Tiny Dungeon" (**CC0**), <https://kenney.nl>.

## Synth World: Singing Grove
- Tree creatures, bee and plants from **Quaternius** (**CC0**), <https://quaternius.com>.
